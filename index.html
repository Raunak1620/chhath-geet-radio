      यह जगह

      About

      Chhath Geet Radio is a free page for the songs and the four days around Chhath Puja. Press play on the TV, keep the river nearby and wait for the sun.

      टीवी पर गीत

      A retro TV on the river bank plays Chhath geet one after another. Open TV mode to fill a big screen at home or at your pandal.

      छठ की कथा

      Chhath honours Surya Dev, the visible source of life, and Chhathi Maiya, whom many families know as Shashthi, the mother who protects children.

      Traditions trace the vrat to the Ramayana, where Sita is said to have kept it, and to the Mahabharata, where Draupadi is said to have fasted for her family's well-being. Families keep their own stories, passed down in the songs sung at the ghat.

      चार दिन

      Chhath Puja 2026 · Nov 13 to 16

      1. Fri13 Nov

        नहाय-खाय

        Nahay Khay

        The vrati bathes, cleans the home and eats one simple meal of kaddu-bhaat and chana dal.

      2. Sat14 Nov

        खरना

        Kharna

        A day-long fast ends at sunset with jaggery kheer and roti. The 36-hour waterless fast begins.

      3. Sun15 Nov

        संध्या अर्घ्य

        Sandhya Arghya

        Families carry the soop with thekua and fruit to the ghat and offer arghya to the setting sun.

      4. Mon16 Nov

        उषा अर्घ्य

        Usha Arghya

        Arghya to the rising sun at dawn. The fast is then broken with prasad.

      Dates follow the 2026 panchang listings. Sunset and sunrise arghya times change from city to city, so check your local panchang for the exact minute.

      घाट पर

      At the ghat

      सुरक्षा

      Go in a group, keep children close to an adult near the water, and follow the barriers and guidance of local volunteers and authorities.

      स्वच्छता

      Carry your puja waste back or use the collection points. Avoid plastic, and keep the river and the path clean for the next family.

      समय

      Reach the ghat early, carry a torch for the dark morning, and keep warm clothes handy. Early November mornings are cold.

      सवाल-जवाब

      FAQ

      What is Chhath Puja?

      Chhath is a four-day festival dedicated to Surya Dev and Chhathi Maiya, celebrated mainly in Bihar, Jharkhand, eastern Uttar Pradesh and Nepal's Terai. Devotees fast and offer arghya to the setting and rising sun.

      How do I show this on a TV?

      Open this page in your smart TV's browser, or cast your browser tab to the TV, then press the TV mode button. The remote's arrow keys change songs, and OK or Space plays and pauses.

      Where do the songs come from?

      Songs play through YouTube's own embedded player, from the official channels of the singers and labels. Nothing is hosted on this site, and the credit for each song is shown in the player.

      Who is Sharda Sinha?

      Sharda Sinha was a folk singer from Bihar, widely called the voice of Chhath. Her Bhojpuri, Maithili and Magahi geet are played at ghats every year.
